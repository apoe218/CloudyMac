About

I joined Zehl & Associates with nearly three years of pre-litigation experience and a record of negotiating favorable settlements for injured clients. I'm driven by a deep commitment to standing up for people who have been wronged by the negligence of others.

I'm a native of the Rio Grande Valley and bilingual in English and Spanish, which lets me advocate for clients and their families in the language they know best. I earned my bachelor's degree in history, with a minor in Business Administration, from Texas A&M University, then got my first taste of the courtroom working for the Hidalgo County Criminal District Attorney's Office before committing to law full time.

I earned my Juris Doctor from Thurgood Marshall School of Law at Texas Southern University, where I served as a Telephone Access to Justice advocate with Texas RioGrande Legal Aid, guiding low-income Texans through the legal system and connecting the most urgent cases with the attorneys who could take them on. Since being licensed in 2023, I've devoted my practice to pre-litigation, working directly with clients and insurance adjusters to resolve cases efficiently and secure maximum recoveries.

Outside the office, I enjoy traveling, photography, and spending time with friends and family.
