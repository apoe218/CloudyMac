Yes, our Halloweens have trended a little warmer, and this year will be near the top

Space City Weather · 10h ago

In brief: In today’s post we discuss Houston’s typical high temperatures on Halloween, and what conditions will be like this year. Also, is there another decently strong cold front coming through any time soon?

The first thing to know about temperatures in Houston on Halloween is that they bounce around a lot. At this time of year we can still feel almost summer-like outside, and we can get some really robust cold fronts. The historical range of highs on October 31 in Houston runs from a low maximum temperature of 47 degrees (set in 1925) to 88 degrees (2004).

Over the last century, the average high temperature has trended about 1 to 2 degrees warmer. Normal highs are now in the upper 70s. This year will be on the warmer side, with highs likely in the low- to mid-80s. I don’t think we’ll approach the the record high of 88 degrees, but it will feel fairly muggy outside. Anyway, by trick-or-treating time temperatures should have fallen into the upper 70s, so it will be pretty comfortable unless you’ve got a warm, woolly costume.
