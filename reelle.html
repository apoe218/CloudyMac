    How to play

    Every game has a secret theme and a mystery film that fits it. You get a vague clue about the theme up front, and clearer ones as you guess.

    • Guess any film. You have 10 guesses.
    • Each guess shows how it compares: year, genre, director, stars, studio, rating, runtime.
    • The Theme column tells you if your guess also fits the game's theme. The mystery film always does.
    • Clue 2 unlocks after 4 guesses. The theme's real name unlocks after 7.
    • A new daily game is picked each day. Use "New random game" for more.
