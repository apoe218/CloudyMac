    How to play

    Every game has a secret theme and a mystery film that fits it. You get a vague clue about the theme, and clearer ones as you guess.

    • Guess any film. You have 10 guesses.
    • Each guess is compared on year, genre, director, cast, studio, rating and runtime. Arrows point toward the answer.
    • Cast lists the lead (★) and supporting actors. Green = same lead. Amber = shared actors, including ones who are the lead in one film and support in the other.
    • The Theme column says whether your guess also fits the game's theme. The mystery film always does.
    • Clue 2 unlocks after 4 guesses. The theme's real name unlocks after 7.
    • A new daily game appears each day. "New random game" gives you as many extras as you like.
